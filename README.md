# frisor-booking-system
Streamlines operations by automating tasks like appointment confirmation, sending reminders to reduce no-shows, and providing a 24/7 booking option for clients. The system benefits both clients, by offering convenient scheduling, and salon owners, by saving time and administrative work. 

Key features
1. Online Booking: Clients can book appointments anytime, anywhere, via a website, social media, or a mobile app.

2. Automated Reminders: Sends automatic SMS or email notifications to clients to reduce no-shows.

3. Calendar Management: Helps staff manage their schedules and prevents double bookings by providing a real-time view of available slots.

4. Client Management: Stores client profiles, preferences, and booking history, which allows for more personalized service.

5. Online Payments: Allows salons to securely accept deposits or full payments online during the booking process.
Service and Staff Selection: Lets clients choose a specific service (e.g., haircut, coloring) and a particular stylist.

6. Business Analytics: Provides reports on booking trends, peak times, and client behavior to help improve business performance.
